Drop Zone Operator – Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026. This policy covers the Drop Zone Operator phone app and the Drop Zone Operator PC app.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We do not run servers that receive your data.

What stays on your device

Your settings, alert choices, quest checklist and chat history are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Optional services you turn on

  • AI answers. If you add your own AI key (for example from Groq), the question you type or speak and short game reference text are sent to that provider to write an answer. Their privacy policy applies to that request.
  • Web search (PC app). If you add a search key (for example Tavily), your question is sent to that provider.
  • Voice (PC app). Push-to-talk audio is turned into text on your own computer. Audio is not uploaded.
  • Screen scan (PC app). When you press the scan hotkey, one screenshot is sent to the AI vision provider you set up, to identify what is on screen. Nothing is captured unless you press the key.
  • Phone link. If you link the phone app to your PC, questions travel over your own home network to your computer.

Game schedule

The app reads the public map-condition schedule from the game's official website. No personal data is sent.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
